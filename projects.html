Diga: Graph Diffusion for Explainable Anti‑Money Laundering

Banking

The Diga project is a flagship initiative under the Joint Laboratory of HKUST and WeBank, aiming to achieve high-recall, interpretable anti-money laundering monitoring using Graph Neural Networks. Ourteam introduces a novel guided graph diffusion method based on subgraph reconstruction to identify suspicious accounts in transaction networks at a scale of 200 million accounts.

Olli: Digital Twin Platform for Smart Vehicles Based on Industrial Internet of Things

Industrial Software

The Olli project focuses on developing an industrial software solution for the operation and maintenance of smart vehicles. Leveraging Digital Twin and Industrial Internet of Things technologies, the system provides real-time monitoring of autonomous vehicles, including driving metrics, location, and battery status. By integrating 3D design data, simulation tools, and AI models, it analyzes and predicts potential safety hazards, thereby achieving comprehensive prognostics and health management.

OPTIMA: Agent-Centric Database System

Database

Traditional database systems are constrained by passive "query–execute–return" models. Our Agent DB Research Group now launches OPTIMA project, which advocates transitioning from static data repositories to a task-driven, agent-centric database architecture. Designed to support the perception, planning, and execution of database operations by autonomous agents, OPTIMA empowers proactive and efficient data interaction.
